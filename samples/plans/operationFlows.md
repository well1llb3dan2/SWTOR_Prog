**Health Pools & Difficulty**
| Difficulty | Combat Tuning | Exclusive Mechanics |
|---|---|---|
| Story Mode (8M / 16M) | Egg Management Baseline | Egg Stomping & Knight Guard Cleave |
| Veteran Mode (8M / 16M) | High Output | Royal Slaughter Enrage |
| Master Mode (8M / 16M) | Not Available | Not Implemented |

**Associated Adds**
* **Caustic Drone:** Acid-spitting adds that spawn from uncrushed eggs.
* **Knight Guard:** Heavy guards requiring dedicated off-tank separation.
* **Pheromone Egg:** Eggs that must be stomped by players before hatching.

**Encounter Phases**
1. **Phase 1: Egg Stomp & Off-Tanking** (`Egg Crushing / Guard Separation`) — Trigger: Pull. Step on eggs before they hatch while burning down Knight Guards.
2. **Phase 2: Caustic Scream & Submersion** (`Aerial Burrow / Acid Carpet`) — Trigger: 40% HP; Queen leaps to the ceiling and floods the chamber with caustic spray.

**Wipe Signatures & Fail Mechanics**
* ⚠️ **Royal Guard Enrage**: Failing to eliminate Knight Guards within their active window causes them to enrage and one-shot tanks.

**End Conditions**
* **Victory Event:** Boss Defeat | **Condition:** Destruction of Mutated Geonosian Queen
